# Draw on the page and capture it

Press **D**, or click the pen button, to draw over the page:

- pens, highlighter and neon
- arrows, boxes and ellipses
- text and numbered callouts
- blur to hide private data

Drawings stay in place while you scroll.

**Screenshot drawing area** captures only the part of the page around your drawing, with the drawing on it. The camera menu also has viewport, full-page, region and element screenshots. The file path is copied, ready to paste into an agent.
